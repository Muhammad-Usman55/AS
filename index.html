Our Story

Born From a
Love of Leather

AS. was founded in 2020 with a simple belief: every woman deserves a bag that is both beautiful and built to last. We partner directly with artisan ateliers to bring you bags that marry heritage craftsmanship with contemporary design.

From the tanneries of Italy to the workshops of our skilled craftspeople, every stitch, every zipper, every piece of hardware is chosen with intention. We believe quality should never be compromised.
